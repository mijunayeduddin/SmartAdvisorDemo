## 5. IMPORTANT RULES (Official Notes from Course Map)

1. **ENG102 → ENG103**: You can take ENG103 only if ENG102 is waived or completed first.
2. **Mandatory Language**: ENG103 & ENG105 are mandatory for all NSU UG students.
3. **BEN205 & HIS103**: Mandatory for all NSU UG students from **Batch 163 and onwards**.
4. **Batch 152–162 Students**: Can take any Humanities course (HIS101/HIS102/HIS103) for 3-credit requirement.
5. **ENG102 Waiver (Batch 152–162)**: Must do ENG111 to maintain 9 language credits.
6. **Elective Pool**: MIC201, MIC309, MIC311, MIC318, MIC404, MIC416, MIC417, MIC418 (8 courses total).
7. **Minimum Electives**: Must complete at least **3 elective courses** out of the 8.
8. **Free Electives**: Encouraged to take 3 free elective courses from the elective pool to strengthen Microbiology knowledge.
